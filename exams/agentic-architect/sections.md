# Exam sections — Professional Agentic Architect

The five sections of Google's
[exam guide](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/professional_agentic_architect_exam_guide_english.pdf),
with their share of the exam, checked 2 Nov 2026. The quiz reads the lines below at run time for
its section titles and weights, so keep their shape: `Section N (title, ~X%)`.

- Section 1 (Building agents using low-code tools, ~13%)
- Section 2 (Using coding agents for application development, ~17%)
- Section 3 (Developing custom agents, ~33%)
- Section 4 (Evaluating and deploying agentic workflows, ~22%)
- Section 5 (Securing and governing agentic workflows, ~15%)
